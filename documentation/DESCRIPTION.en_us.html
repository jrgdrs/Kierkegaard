Kierkegaard is a modern serif typeface in renaissance style that merges historical grace with contemporary precision. Drawing inspiration from the philosophical spirit of Søren Aabye Kierkegaard (1813–1855).

The complete family contains 40 members in five weights, each paired with a matching italic. Additional features include old-style figures, small capitals, and refined character detailing for professional typographic versatility. And also a distinctive display version for headlines and an text version for bodytexts.

To contribute, see github.com/jrgdrs/Kierkegaard.
